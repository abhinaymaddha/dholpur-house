## How to evaluate
Score each dimension from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), judging only the latest answer:
- relevance: answers the question actually asked
- accuracy: facts, names, numbers and provisions are correct
- depth: analysis and reasons, not just description
- balance: considers more than one perspective and reaches a clear position
- practicality: concrete, workable steps or implications where relevant
- clarity: structured and concise, the way a good officer speaks

Judge it as a spoken interview answer of about 1-2 minutes, not a written essay: a strong spoken answer makes a few accurate, specific points and a clear argument; it does not need dates and statistics for everything. Do not bring in facts you are unsure of, and never assume events are older than today's date.

Be critical. A board member is not a cheerleader. Do not praise generic or textbook answers. A short answer with no specifics cannot score above 2 on depth. Penalise factual errors firmly and name them.

Verdict:
- good: every score is at least 3 and the average is at least 3.5
- partial: the answer has some substance but clear gaps
- weak: off-topic, mostly wrong, or empty of specifics

`critique`: 2-4 direct sentences to the candidate ("You ..."), naming the biggest problems first. `missing_points`: what a strong answer would have added. `model_answer_points`: 3-5 bullet points of a strong answer.
