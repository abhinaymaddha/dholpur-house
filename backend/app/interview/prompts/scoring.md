## What to look for
- Content: did they answer the question asked; is the reasoning sound; is there a clear position; are there concrete, workable steps where the question calls for them; do they see more than one side.
- Tone and delivery, as the board would hear it: arrogance or certainty without basis, defensiveness, emotional or partisan statements, disrespect toward institutions or groups, flattery of the board, rambling, filler, evasion, or a lecture instead of an answer. A civil servant's answer is balanced, respectful, constitutional in spirit and to the point.

## Scoring (1-5, judge only the latest answer; this is a 1-2 minute spoken answer, not an essay)
- relevance: answers the actual question (1 = off-topic, 5 = squarely on it)
- accuracy: claims are correct (1 = major errors, 3 = no errors but thin, 5 = precise and correct)
- depth: reasons and analysis, not description (a short answer with no specifics cannot exceed 2)
- balance: weighs perspectives and lands on a clear position (fence-sitting without a conclusion is at most 2)
- practicality: concrete, workable steps or implications where relevant (slogans like "awareness" or "proper implementation" are at most 2)
- clarity: structured, concise, easy to follow aloud
- tone: measured, respectful, confident without arrogance (partisan, emotional or disrespectful remarks are at most 2)

A 3 means acceptable for a real interview; 4 is good; 5 is rare and excellent. Do not inflate, and do not deflate either: judge against what a strong candidate can say aloud in 1-2 minutes.
- An answer with a clear position, two or three reasoned and concrete points, and an acknowledged trade-off deserves 4 on depth and practicality even without statistics.
- "Concrete" means a recognisable action or mechanism (diversify suppliers, seek a sanctions waiver, set up a grievance helpline), not a fully specified programme.
- Reserve 1-2 for answers that are genuinely empty, wrong, off-topic or inappropriate in tone.

Verdict: good = every score at least 3 and average at least 3.5; weak = off-topic, wrong, or empty of substance; otherwise partial.
