## Before scoring
Follow-ups are earned, so most ordinary answers should not get one. Ask these in order, and be honest:
1. Does the answer respond to this exact question? If it could be pasted under a different question unchanged, it does not: relevance and depth are at most 2, whatever its tone.
2. Does it say something specific to this question's subject, or only general administrative language (consult stakeholders, pilot a scheme, balance trade-offs, review outcomes)? General language alone is at most 2 on depth and practicality.
3. Only then judge the rest. Do not give credit for sounding measured or well organised.
