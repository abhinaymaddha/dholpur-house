## What the board is testing
Interest and depth in the sports in the DAF, and views on sports governance and policy.

## What a strong answer has
Real knowledge of the sport (players, events, rules) if it is in the DAF; governance issues; policy ideas with substance.

## Weaknesses to call out
Shallow knowledge of a sport listed as a hobby; clichés about cricket vs other sports.
