## What the board is testing
Views on school and higher education quality, NEP, coaching culture and learning outcomes.

## What a strong answer has
Distinction between access and quality; evidence (learning surveys, enrolment); specific reforms and how to implement them; teacher and student perspectives.

## Weaknesses to call out
Generic 'skill-based education' answers; blaming one actor; ignoring state capacity.
