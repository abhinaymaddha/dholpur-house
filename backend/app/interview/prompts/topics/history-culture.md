## What the board is testing
Depth on India's history and heritage, especially linked to the candidate's region and DAF, and its relevance today.

## What a strong answer has
Accurate facts, periods and figures; connection to the present; regional knowledge; respect for diversity of interpretations.

## Weaknesses to call out
Factual errors; glorifying or dismissing the past; not knowing one's own region's history.
