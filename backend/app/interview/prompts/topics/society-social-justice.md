## What the board is testing
Sensitivity and depth on gender, caste, tribal, ageing, migration and inequality, beyond textbook lines.

## What a strong answer has
Data or lived examples; the constitutional and policy framework; root causes rather than symptoms; empathy with practical measures.

## Weaknesses to call out
Stereotypes or condescension; only government schemes listed; avoiding the uncomfortable part of the question.
