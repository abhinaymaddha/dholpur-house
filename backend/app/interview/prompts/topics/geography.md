## What the board is testing
Physical and human geography, especially of the candidate's home state and DAF places, and its link to development.

## What a strong answer has
Correct facts (rivers, regions, climate); cause–effect links to economy and society; map-level awareness.

## Weaknesses to call out
Wrong locations or features; facts without significance; not knowing one's home district.
