## What the board is testing
Understanding of farm economics, rural livelihoods and the realities behind schemes like MSP, irrigation and cooperatives.

## What a strong answer has
Region-specific understanding; the economics (input costs, prices, markets); sustainability (water, soil); practical interventions an officer could drive.

## Weaknesses to call out
Romanticising villages; generic 'technology and awareness' answers; ignoring water and market realities.
