## What the board is testing
How the candidate would run things as an officer: implementation, accountability, citizen focus and working within rules.

## What a strong answer has
Concrete administrative steps (who, what, when); awareness of real schemes and their delivery problems; accountability and grievance mechanisms; technology used sensibly.

## Weaknesses to call out
Slogans ('awareness', 'proper implementation') without mechanism; ignoring constraints like staff, budget or law; heroics that bypass procedure.
