## What the board is testing
Whether the candidate truly knows their own background: hometown, education, job and hobbies, and can connect them to administration.

## What a strong answer has
Specific depth on every DAF item (a hobby means real knowledge); links from background to public service; honesty about gaps.

## Weaknesses to call out
Not knowing basics of one's own degree, job or hometown; inflated hobbies; generic connections to governance.
