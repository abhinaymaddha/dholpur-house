## What the board is testing
Integrity and judgement in dilemmas, and whether the candidate's values are consistent and practical.

## What a strong answer has
The values in conflict named clearly; stakeholders considered; a decision with justification; awareness of rules and consequences.

## Weaknesses to call out
Moral lectures; refusing to choose; choices that break rules casually; inconsistency with earlier answers.
