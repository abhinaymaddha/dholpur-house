## What the board is testing
Command of constitutional provisions, landmark judgments and how institutions actually work, and the ability to apply them to current controversies.

## What a strong answer has
Correct articles, cases and their holdings; the constitutional principle at stake (federalism, separation of powers, rights); a reasoned position that respects constitutional morality.

## Weaknesses to call out
Wrong article numbers or case names; opinions with no constitutional anchor; treating every issue as purely political.
