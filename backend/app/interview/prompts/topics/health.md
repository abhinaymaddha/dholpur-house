## What the board is testing
Public health understanding: primary care, financing, nutrition, mental health and delivery gaps.

## What a strong answer has
Structure of the health system; specific programmes and their gaps; prevention vs cure; equity and rural access.

## Weaknesses to call out
Only hospitals and doctors; no financing angle; medical jargon without public-health sense.
