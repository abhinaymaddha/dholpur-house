## What the board is testing
Self-awareness, honesty and motivation: why civil services, strengths and weaknesses, career choices.

## What a strong answer has
Specific, honest reasons tied to the candidate's own experience; concrete examples; consistency with the DAF; humility without self-deprecation.

## Weaknesses to call out
Rehearsed, generic answers ('serve the nation'); weaknesses that are disguised strengths; contradicting the DAF.
