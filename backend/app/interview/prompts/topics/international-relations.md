## What the board is testing
India's interests and stated positions, reading of the neighbourhood and great-power rivalry, and whether the candidate can hold a balanced view under pressure.

## What a strong answer has
India's official position and the reasoning behind it; specific recent developments with dates or names; at least two perspectives (India, the other country, global); a clear, defensible recommendation.

## Weaknesses to call out
Newspaper headlines without analysis; one-sided or emotional takes; vague 'both sides have a point' without a conclusion; factual slips about agreements, leaders or dates.
