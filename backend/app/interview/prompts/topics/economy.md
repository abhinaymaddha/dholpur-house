## What the board is testing
Grasp of macro indicators, recent policy moves and trade-offs, and the ability to explain them simply.

## What a strong answer has
Correct concepts and recent numbers (growth, inflation, deficit, policy rates); the trade-off involved (growth vs inflation, revenue vs relief); effects on ordinary people and on states.

## Weaknesses to call out
Jargon without explanation; outdated or invented figures; ignoring distributional effects; single-cause explanations.
