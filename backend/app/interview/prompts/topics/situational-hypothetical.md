## What the board is testing
Administrative judgement under pressure: what the candidate would actually do as a DM, SP or secretary.

## What a strong answer has
Immediate steps in sequence; legal powers and limits; stakeholders involved; balancing urgency with due process; follow-up and accountability.

## Weaknesses to call out
Superhero answers; skipping the law or procedure; only 'I will form a committee'; ignoring the human impact.
