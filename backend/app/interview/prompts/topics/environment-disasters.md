## What the board is testing
Balancing development with sustainability, India's climate commitments, and disaster preparedness on the ground.

## What a strong answer has
Specific commitments and frameworks (NDCs, net zero target, Sendai); local examples; the development–environment trade-off stated honestly; preparedness and response steps.

## Weaknesses to call out
Moralising without trade-offs; confusing climate terms; no mention of communities affected.
