## What the board is testing
Strategic thinking about internal and external security, civil–military aspects and India's posture.

## What a strong answer has
Specific threats and how they evolved; institutional responses (forces, intelligence, laws); balance between security and rights; restraint in language.

## Weaknesses to call out
Jingoism; revealing poor facts about operations or forces; ignoring the political and development dimension of internal security.
