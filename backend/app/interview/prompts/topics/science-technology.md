## What the board is testing
Awareness of recent advances (space, AI, semiconductors, biotech) and their governance and ethical questions.

## What a strong answer has
Accurate basic explanation of the technology; India's programmes and position; risks, regulation and equity; a practical use in governance.

## Weaknesses to call out
Buzzwords without understanding; uncritical techno-optimism; outdated facts about missions or policies.
