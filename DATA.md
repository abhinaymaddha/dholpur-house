# About the data

## Credit

Every interview transcript in this project comes from the public Telegram channel
**[Interview Transcripts (CSPT-2026)](https://t.me/csetranscripts)** (@csetranscripts).

All credit goes to the channel's admins, who collect and publish them, and to the candidates who
wrote down and shared their UPSC Personality Test experiences for others to learn from. This
project only organises that material so it can be searched, read and practised with. It claims
no ownership of it.

## What is included

- `backend/seed/csetranscripts_messages.jsonl`: the text of the channel's messages (no media),
  one JSON object per message, as exported with Telegram Desktop.
- Email addresses and phone numbers are removed automatically, here and in every ingest.
- The transcripts are **not** covered by this repository's license (CC BY-NC 4.0 applies to the
  code and documentation only). They remain the work of their authors.

## Removal

If you are the channel admin or the author of a transcript and you'd like it removed (or the whole
dataset taken out of this repository), please open an issue at
https://github.com/abhinaymaddha/upsc-prep/issues or contact @abhinaymaddha on GitHub, and it will
be removed promptly. No questions asked.

## Using it responsibly

Candidates often posted under pseudonyms. Please don't use this project to identify them or attach
real identities to their transcripts.
